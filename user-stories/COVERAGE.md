Package tests linked from individual stories are supporting evidence only. `index.ts`, `src/admission/main.ts`, `docs/guide/features.md` and the [HTTP route reference](../docs/reference/endpoints.md) were reviewed to reconcile the inventory. No runtime behavior is asserted.
