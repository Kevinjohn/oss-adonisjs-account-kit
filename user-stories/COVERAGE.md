The stories cover package exports and configuration; password reset, registration/verification, authentication and account settings; invitation lifecycle; optional magic links, sessions, export/deletion and social identity actions; admission status/preflight, decisions, gating and operator lifecycle; managed routes/OpenAPI; setup, diagnostics, pruning, login-retirement review and operator commands; user adapters, adapter contract tests, notification delivery and in-memory notification capture. Individual test links are supporting source evidence only. Root/admission entry maps and the [feature guide](../docs/guide/features.md), [endpoint reference](../docs/reference/endpoints.md) and examples were reviewed to reconcile the inventory. No runtime behaviour is asserted.
