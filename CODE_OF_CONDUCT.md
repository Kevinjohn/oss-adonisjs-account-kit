# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, accept feedback on your work gracefully, and do not
harass anyone in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer at
[cve@kevinjohngallagher.com](mailto:cve@kevinjohngallagher.com), the private address also used for
security reports. Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this
code, and may block a contributor temporarily or permanently. Enforcement follows the
[Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
